How each setup is defined

Reversal

Based on MCD (+349%) and DAL (+253%). In the last 7 days RSI hit 33 or lower and the close sat 5% or more below the 50-day SMA. Triggered: MACD crossed above its signal in the last 2 days. Turned: crossed 3 to 7 days ago, histogram still positive, RSI under 60 and close still at or below the 50-day. Watch: histogram below zero but rising. Early: oversold and histogram still falling.

Momentum

Based on AAPL (+116%). Pullback in an uptrend: RSI 45 to 60, MACD above zero and above its signal, close 0 to 3% above the 50-day SMA.

Squeeze

Bollinger bandwidth in the tightest 20% of the last 6 months, close within 3% of the 20-day high and above the 50-day SMA.

Avoid

Based on UBER (−26%). RSI 38 to 46, MACD histogram flat near zero, close 4% or more below the 50-day SMA. A slow drift where time decay eats calls.

Only stocks above $10 with at least $100M average daily dollar volume are scanned. A stock that fits more than one setup shows only the first, in the order above. These are rule matches, not predictions or advice.
